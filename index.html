WHAT IT DOES

Built for Everyday Use

Simple, reliable features that make downloading and listening straightforward.

Audio & Video Downloads

Paste any YouTube link or search within the app to download media in your preferred format: high-quality MP3 audio or MP4 video files.

Ambient Soundscapes

Stream soothing ambient sounds (Monsoon Rain, Forest, Neon Cyberpunk, Coffee Shop) without interruptions to help you study, work, or sleep.

Sleep Timer Auto-Stop

Fall asleep peacefully. Set the sleep timer to 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes, and the playback will automatically fade out and stop.

Downloads File Manager

Manage your downloaded media right inside the app. Play files instantly with the built-in mini-player, see file sizes, and delete files when needed.

Auto Clipboard Detection

Copy a link from YouTube or any browser, open Zen Mate, and the app immediately recognizes the link so you can start downloading with one tap.

Files Stay on Your Phone

All downloaded music and videos are saved directly onto your device storage. No account is required and no personal download history is collected.

HOW TO INSTALL

Install in 3 Simple Steps

Because Zen Mate downloads media directly, it is provided via direct Android APK install.

1

Download APK

Tap the download button to save zen-mate-v1.0.0.apk directly to your device.

2

Allow Unknown Sources

If prompted by Android, tap "Settings" and toggle "Allow from this source" for your browser or file manager.

3

Install & Open

Tap Install, grant storage access when asked, and you are ready to download media and listen to ambient audio.
