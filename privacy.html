Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Effective Immediately

Welcome to Zen Mate ("the Application"), developed by Zentix Studio and founded by Kalana Sahan Dillimuni ("we", "us", or "our"). We believe privacy is a fundamental right. Zen Mate is built with a privacy-first architecture: your downloaded media, bookmarks, and preferences never leave your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Zen Mate does not collect or store:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (such as name, email address, phone number, or physical location).
  • Your download history, search terms, or URLs pasted into the application.
  • Your music playlists or media library contents.

2. Local Device Storage and File Access

Zen Mate requires Android storage permissions (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android versions) strictly to:

  • Save downloaded audio files (MP3/M4A) and video files (MP4) to your device's Music, Movies, or Download folders.
  • Scan and calculate storage usage within the Zen Mate settings screen so you can manage your device space.

All downloaded files remain 100% stored on your local hardware.

3. Anonymous Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

To ensure app stability and fix technical errors across various Android hardware configurations, the application utilizes Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC). Crashlytics collects only anonymous diagnostic technical data:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and Android OS version.
  • Application build number and stack traces at the exact moment of an unexpected crash.

This technical data cannot be used to identify individual users.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Streams

When you stream ambient soundscapes or query YouTube media links, network requests are made directly between your Android device and the content host. Zen Mate acts solely as a client-side media processor and does not proxy or log your internet traffic on any proprietary server.

5. Security of Your Information

Because we do not maintain a cloud database or user accounts for Zen Mate, there is no centralized database of user records to compromise. All media data and cached preferences remain under your control on your physical device.
