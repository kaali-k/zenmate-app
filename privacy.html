Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Effective Immediately

Welcome to Zen Mate ("the Application"), developed by Zentix Team and founded by Kalana Sahan Dillimuni ("we", "us", or "our"). Zen Mate is designed with a privacy-first approach: your downloaded files and preferences remain stored directly on your personal Android device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Zen Mate does not collect or track:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (name, email, phone number, or location).
  • Your media download history, search keywords, or URLs pasted into the app.
  • Your offline audio playlists or media library contents.

2. Storage Permissions and Local Files

Zen Mate requires Android storage permissions strictly to:

  • Save downloaded audio files (MP3/M4A) and video files (MP4) directly into your device's Music, Movies, or Download folders.
  • Scan and calculate storage usage within the Zen Mate settings screen so you can inspect and clean your device space.

All downloaded media files remain 100% on your local storage.

3. Anonymous Diagnostics

To ensure app stability and troubleshoot bugs, the application uses Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC). Crashlytics collects anonymous diagnostic technical data:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and Android version.
  • Crash stack traces when an unexpected error occurs.

This technical data is strictly anonymous and cannot identify you personally.
