Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Effective for Google Play Distribution

Welcome to Zen Mate: Video Downloader & Player ("the Application"), developed and distributed by Zentix Team ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the official Zen Mate application available through the Google Play Store.

Core Privacy Principle: Zen Mate is built with a zero-tracking, privacy-first architecture. We do not require accounts, logins, or personal profiling. All media downloaded or played remains solely on your personal Android device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Zen Mate does not collect, process, or sell any of the following:

  • Personal Identity: Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or home address.
  • User Activity: Search keywords, clipboard history, or individual URLs processed within the application.
  • Media Library Content: The names, contents, or metadata of your personal audio/video files.
  • Precise Location: GPS coordinates or real-time location tracking.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

Zen Mate requests strictly necessary permissions required to deliver its core media playback and saving functionalities:

  • Scoped Media Access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): Used exclusively to enable the offline audio/video player to locate media saved by Zen Mate and display them in your library. Zen Mate does not require or utilize MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display the persistent media playback controller (play/pause/skip) on your lock screen and notification shade when playing ambient soundscapes or music in the background.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch streaming data and user-requested public media content from online sources.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used for subtle haptic feedback when interacting with app controls.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The Application utilizes trusted third-party diagnostic SDKs strictly for crash diagnosis and performance monitoring in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics (Google LLC): Helps our development team monitor stability, diagnose app crashes, and understand aggregate usage trends. Information transmitted is strictly non-personally identifiable technical telemetry (e.g., device model, OS version, stack traces).

4. Advertising Policy: 100% Ad-Free Experience

Zen Mate Official is currently completely ad-free. The application contains no advertising SDKs, does not display banner or interstitial advertisements, and does not collect or request the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).

5. Google Play Policy & Intellectual Property Compliance

Zen Mate operates in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and third-party terms of service:

  • No YouTube Downloads: In strict compliance with Google Play Policies and the YouTube Terms of Service, Zen Mate does not download, convert, extract, or save audiovisual content from YouTube.
  • Authorized Media Only: Users are solely responsible for respecting the copyright, intellectual property rights, and terms of service of any third-party platforms from which media is saved.

5. Children's Privacy

Zen Mate is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe any such data has inadvertently been transmitted, please notify us immediately.

6. Security of Your Data

Because all downloaded files, playlist items, and ambient mixer settings are stored locally on your device storage, you retain complete control over your media files. You may delete them at any time using Zen Mate or your device's built-in File Manager.
