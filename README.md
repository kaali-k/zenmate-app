## Features

- **Audio & Video Downloader**: Download YouTube audio in high-quality MP3 format or videos in MP4 directly to your device storage.
- **Ambient Soundscapes**: Stream curated offline-capable ambient sounds (Rain, Forest, Neon Lo-Fi, Cafe) without ad interruptions.
- **Sleep Timer**: Built-in 15m, 30m, 45m, and 60m sleep timer with automatic fadeout.
- **Downloads Manager**: View, play, and manage your downloaded audio and video files with file size inspection and deletion tools.
- **Auto Clipboard Detection**: Automatically detects copied YouTube links when opening the app.
- **Background Playback**: Keep listening to ambient sounds or downloaded tracks with system notification drawer controls.
- **Privacy-First**: No account required, no download tracking. Files stay on your local Android device.

## Download & Installation

1. Download **[zen-mate-v1.0.0.apk](https://github.com/kaali-k/zenmate-app/releases/latest/download/zen-mate-v1.0.0.apk)**.
2. If prompted by Android, tap **Settings** and enable **Allow from this source**.
3. Tap **Install** and open Zen Mate.
4. Grant storage permissions to save your media files.
